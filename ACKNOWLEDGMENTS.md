# Acknowledgments

ResearchBoost began as a standalone fork of the creator's **Survey to Slides** artifact-template skill and its multi-agent robotics research talk. The original installed skill was left unchanged.

**Pascale Fung's EMNLP 2026 keynote inspired the creator to understand a research field through its history**: how objectives, assumptions and constraints changed, and why successive ideas became necessary. The official program lists *A Tale of Two Objectives: Past, Present and Future of NLP*.

**Graham Neubig's EMNLP 2026 keynote helped the creator choose criteria for evaluating research impact** and shaped the project's frontier-selection approach. The official program lists *On the Value of Open Research in Language Modeling - An Adversarial Dialog*.

Both keynotes are credited on the [official EMNLP 2026 keynote page](https://2026.emnlp.org/program/keynotes/), checked on 13 November 2026.

These statements acknowledge the creator's inspiration. ResearchBoost's five operational criteria are an adaptation by the creator, not a verbatim quotation or a checklist verified from a keynote transcript. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Thanks also to the authors, labs, open-source communities, benchmark builders and standards groups cited in the robotics demo. Their original figures, results, names and logos remain attributed to their sources.
