## Promotional narration

The promotional narration was synthesized locally with the generic English `af_heart` voice using [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx) (MIT runtime) and [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) (Apache 2.0 model). It does not imitate either acknowledged keynote speaker. Model weights and Python dependencies are not bundled. [Production metadata](examples/robotics-coordination/promo/production.json) records the voice and model hashes used for this promotional asset.
