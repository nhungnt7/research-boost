# Delivery checks

1. The question, scope, cutoff and source coverage are explicit; inaccessible sources and untested claims are identified.
2. Slide 02's numbered agenda describes what each section covers. Presenter Perspective appears only if supplied or confirmed.
3. History connects constraints, milestones and unresolved limits. Frontier selection uses five criteria with per-work evidence.
4. Each technical work has a readable mechanism and result with named comparisons, tasks, scale and limitations.
5. Counts reconcile to deduplicated records and venue/year denominators; partial years and changing coverage are visible. If no auditable corpus exists, omit population-level trend claims.
6. Community entries link to actual dated contributions, classified by release openness rather than institution type.
7. Two future slides connect evidence to questions and questions to concrete experiments. The reading guide is organized by problem.
8. All slides have been rendered and inspected. Native statistics remain editable. Verify exported links and record any untested import/animation behavior.
9. Deliver PPTX/PDF when supported, separate English speaker text, evidence ledger, reading path and relevant corpus files. Keep originals and templates unchanged.
