Use $research-boost to help me get up to speed in a research field.
Start from keywords: [your terms and synonyms]. Topic: [your topic]. Exclude: [out-of-scope meanings]. Build query families, screen relevance, and explain venue and journal coverage before making trend claims.
Audience: researchers entering this field. Make a 30-minute English talk.
Explain the core problem, field map, history and current frontier.
Use the five frontier criteria and include two future slides with testable experiments.
Provide separate English speaker text, a source ledger and a reading path.
Verify sources and record the cutoff. Keep statistics traceable to a dated corpus.
