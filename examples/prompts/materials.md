# Start from your best research materials

Share materials you already find useful: papers, literature reviews or surveys, curated reading lists, notes, course/talk slides, and official research-group links. Replace the bracketed fields and attach or link the actual materials.

```text
Use $research-boost to help me get up to speed on [research field or question].
Start by reading the quality materials I provide:
- Papers and literature reviews: [PDFs, DOI/arXiv links, survey or bibliography].
- Existing materials: [curated reading list, relevant notes, course/talk materials].
- Research groups: [official lab/team pages and relevant public projects].
I find these useful because [their relevance to my question].
I already understand [background]; I want to understand [gaps or open questions].

Use these materials to establish the terminology, problems, mechanisms and history.
Verify claims against primary sources. Search beyond the supplied materials for
missing or competing approaches; use keywords to expand discovery.
Audience: [who]. Duration: [minutes]. Language: English.
Create a source-linked talk, separate speaker text, an evidence ledger and a reading path.
Use the five frontier criteria and two future slides with concrete experiments.
Record the cutoff and source coverage. Omit Presenter Perspective.
```
